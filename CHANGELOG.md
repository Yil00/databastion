## Unreleased

### ✨ Features
- MySQL / MariaDB Audit from the MariaDB `server_audit` log, the Percona `audit_log` / `audit_log_filter` JSON log and `performance_schema`, at most Partial, with the `signature.mysqldump`, `signature.into_outfile`, `shape.full_table_read` and `volume.large_result` signals (#64, ADR-0023)
- Console: `AccessEvent.bytes` stored and shown, target notes stored and rendered from the phrase catalog, signals missing from the registry flagged, `signature.*` signals kept first when an incident's signals are truncated (#62)
- Agent: `TargetStatus.notes` from `check()` in the PostgreSQL and MySQL / MariaDB connectors (at most 16 closed-code notes per target, including the new `audit.records_dropped` code) and the scan coverage counters (`objects_sampled`, `skipped_*`) on the terminal job status, both sent only when the console lists their capability; a `400` for an unknown field on a body carrying a gated field clears the capabilities and the items are sent once more without it (`gated_fields_stripped_total`) (#68, ADR-0022)
- Console: rate limits shared across console processes through PostgreSQL (`rate_limit_counters`, keys stored as HMAC under `rate-limit-keys.v1`): fail closed (`429`, `Retry-After: 5`) for agent authentication, enrollment, rotation, logins and channel test sends when the shared store fails, per-process fallback for ingest rates and audit budgets; dedicated pool, circuit breaker, `rate_limits.prune` worker job, `databastion_console_rate_limit_store_errors_total` and `databastion_console_rate_limit_counters_rows` metrics (#69, ADR-0024)
- Agent: MySQL / MariaDB `check()` evaluates the privileges held through roles with the rules for direct grants: every applicable role on MySQL 8.0.19+ (`SHOW GRANTS FOR CURRENT_USER() USING …`, at most 16 direct and mandatory roles, allow-listed and quoted names), only the current (default) role on MariaDB, other roles reported as `privilege.roles_not_evaluated`, never `SET ROLE`; database grants matched as `LIKE` patterns; privilege lists cut at their limit or with unreadable rows reported as not evaluated (#70, ADR-0025)
- Agent: MongoDB Discovery (P5-A): own minimal wire-protocol client (`OP_MSG`, bounded BSON reader, SCRAM-SHA-256 only, rustls, one declared host without replica-set discovery), `$sample` or natural-order sampling with bounds per document and per collection, normalized field paths (`[]` for arrays, `*` for value-like keys and data-keyed maps), views not sampled, time-series collections read through their view, `check()` with over-privilege and coverage notes and the audit level None; recommended account: a custom `find` + `listCollections` role instead of `read` + `clusterMonitor` (#74, ADR-0026)
- Agent: MongoDB Audit (P5-B, P5-C): the Enterprise / Percona `auditLog` JSON file (Partial once a successful `authCheck` was read in the last 24 h, Limited before), the structured JSON server log or the profiler (Limited), never Full; closed-shape facts only (command documents skipped by a serde visitor, or reduced by a fixed server-side projection on the profiler); `signature.mongodump` / `signature.mongoexport` from the application name, `shape.full_table_read`, `volume.large_result`; the agent's own reads left out within its Discovery budget; `mongodb.audit_log` setting; new notes `audit.auditlog_on_community`, `audit.authcheck_success_pending`, `audit.slow_operations_only`, `audit.source_not_configured`, `audit.limited_pending_first_record` (server log and profiler report None until a record was read in the last 24 h) (#76, ADR-0027)
- Agent: OpenLDAP connector (phase 6): own bounded LDAPv3 client (no write operation encodable, BER lengths checked before the body, result codes only, referrals never followed), LDAPS or StartTLS over rustls (no cleartext on a network), simple bind with a service DN or SASL `EXTERNAL` over `ldapi://`; Discovery of the text attributes of the server schema, custom ones included, never `userPassword` / `authPassword` or other credential hashes, per container with locations (naming context, container, structural object class, attribute); `check()` with the audit level proven per naming context from `cn=accesslog` records (Full only when reads and failed operations are proven logged for every naming context, else Partial, Limited or None) and the `cn=config`, password-attribute and accesslog readability notes (write access noted as not evaluated); Audit from `cn=accesslog` read by `entryCSN`, filters and DNs reduced in memory to closed facts, principals sent as keyed fingerprints except `anonymous`, the agent's own DN and `openldap.clear_principals`, new `shape.bulk_search` signal and paged `volume.large_result`; `openldap` target block in `agent.yaml` (#79, ADR-0029)
- Console: alert when an agent's `dropped_batches` rises: each heartbeat adds the rise of `spool.dropped_batches` to the agent's unalerted count (migration `0031`), turned at most once per agent and hour into an `agent.batches_dropped` security event (`medium`), an audit entry and a notification to the `system_alerts` channels, with console-computed numbers, timestamps and ids only; drops within the hour go in the next alert, raised by the next heartbeat or the worker's minute schedule; the agent page shows the spool counters and the last alerts (#75)
- Console: system-alert budget per channel and UTC hour (`DATABASTION_SYSTEM_ALERTS_MAX_PER_HOUR`, default 20) shared through PostgreSQL, with a per-agent share of `max(2, ceil(limit / 4))`, an hourly `system_alerts.suppressed` digest and a bypass for critical `agent.rotation_conflict` alerts only; `agent.audit_stream_stopped` alert (`medium`) when a target reports `audit.stream_stopped`, repeated every hour while the stream stays stopped; LDAP labels for containers and object classes in the findings and events views (#81, ADR-0031, ADR-0033)
- Agent: Audit read positions and the own-account row counters persist across agent restarts: the MySQL / MariaDB `performance_schema` cursor (with the server's start time) and the MongoDB profiler positions join the file offsets and OpenLDAP CSNs under `<state_dir>/audit/`, and a restart no longer gives a fresh row budget per object (#88)
- Agent: MySQL / MariaDB `check()` evaluates the privileges granted to MariaDB `PUBLIC` (10.11+); heartbeat checks recognize aliases of the same account (default ports, IP literal forms, loopback, socket symlinks, declared host names resolving to a shared address, resolved only for declared targets and cached 5 minutes), a timed-out check takes the last turn of its account, and `checks_account_busy_total` is counted apart from `checks_timed_out_total` (#88)
- Agent: the `performance_schema` and `pg_stat_statements` Audit streams re-probe their prerequisites on the session they hold and close a stale session before reconnecting, so Audit holds one connection at a time (MySQL / MariaDB `MAX_USER_CONNECTIONS` sizing unchanged) (#88)
- Agent: failed-login flood cap: at most 100 named `auth_failure` groups per target and window, a 256-entry folded table, then overflow events per client address with the attempt count (`auth_failures_overflowed_total`), in the agent core for every engine; the spool evicts by class and keeps `signature.*` batches last (`spool_rejected_batches_total`) (#88)
- Agent: stable fuzz targets for the MongoDB (BSON, `OP_MSG`, `auditLog` and server log lines, profiler entries) and OpenLDAP (BER, `LDAPMessage`, search filters, accesslog entries) parsers in `agent/fuzz` (cargo-fuzz, own workspace, `fuzzing` feature never enabled in the agent binary), run 10 s per target by the `agent-fuzz-smoke` CI job (#90)
- Tests: new real-server CI coverage: MongoDB `verify_full` TLS against a TLS-only server with a test CA generated at run time, OpenLDAP SASL `EXTERNAL` over `ldapi://` against the dev slapd, and the MongoDB `auditLog` source against Percona Server for MongoDB 8.0 (opt-in dev service `psmdb`) (#90)
- Agent: Discovery pacing: each object's sampling and each catalog read is charged a pause of `busy × (100 − d) / d` (`d` = `limits.discovery_duty_cycle_percent`, default 1, range 1 to 100, `100` disables it; failed units included), paid before the next unit and never after the last one, i.e. at most about 1 % of one database core per scan, scans running one at a time per agent; a scan that would reach its deadline stops before the next unit, reports the objects left as `skipped_limit` and succeeds; the order is rotated per scan, first the databases or LDAP naming contexts, then the objects inside each; on a console without the coverage counters, such a scan is reported `failed` (`timeout`); pauses end when the scan stops; the agent acknowledges a scan with `running` before starting it and does not run it when the console answers `404` / `409` (#93, ADR-0035 proposed). **Scans now take longer by default**: about 100 times their query time (200 MariaDB tables: about 2 minutes instead of 7 s); size the scan's `max_duration_s` and `limits.max_scan_duration_s` accordingly ([docs/10-user-guide.md](docs/10-user-guide.md#9-discovery-scans-and-findings))
- Console: a queued `discovery.scan` of an online agent (heartbeat within 90 s) is no longer given up after 5 deliveries while within `first_delivered_at + max_duration_s + 600 s`; scan deadlines and the findings window are anchored on the first delivery; a malformed stored `max_duration_s` counts as 900 s; the default scan budget is 3600 s instead of the contract's 900 s, for paced scans (#94)
- Tests: load / database impact harness (`e2e/load/`) with a weekly and on-demand `load` CI job: database CPU impact of Discovery on PostgreSQL, MariaDB and MongoDB, the Audit path under a fixed-rate workload, agent CPU and memory bounds (#92)

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- Console: policy and notification wake-ups were not sent by the production build; process-wide state now lives in `processGlobal` / `processSlot`, and the build checks the wake-up functions (#63)
- Agent: the PostgreSQL connector's own table-less statements are no longer reported as access events; other agent-account events on unknown objects are always reported; pgaudit counts as loaded only when its `pgaudit.log_catalog` setting is in `pg_settings`; `pg_stat_statements` counts as a catalog only in the extension's schema (#65)
- Agent: a scan's terminal status no longer overtakes its findings: it waits up to 120 s for the console to answer the scan's findings batches (no wait for a cancelled scan, a parked `/findings` or a spool worker in backoff), otherwise it is counted in `scan_status_before_flush_total` (#70, ADR-0025)
- Agent: writes, DDL and DCL by the agent's own database account are always reported as access events, on PostgreSQL, MySQL / MariaDB and MongoDB; the own-account exclusion now covers only reads (within the Discovery budget) and connections, since the agent never writes (#76, ADR-0027 decision 7)
- Agent: access event `ts` / `ts_last` are clamped to the agent clock in the core, so a database server clock ahead of the agent no longer gets batches rejected with `400` (`formatMaximum`), raising an integrity event and losing the events; dev and E2E agent accounts raised to 5 connections (ADR-0025 decision 11) (#72)
- Agent: heartbeat target checks run concurrently under one 10 s deadline instead of one after the other (N slow targets delayed the heartbeat by up to N × 10 s), taking turns per database account; a check that misses the deadline is reported `timeout` with `check.timed_out` (#70, ADR-0025)
- Agent: a panic in a connector no longer stops the agent: every connector call (`check`, `discover`, `audit_stream`) runs through a panic guard in the core that fails that call (`connector_panics_total`); an Audit stream that panics is restarted from its persisted cursor and, after 3 panics in a row, stopped (level None, note `audit.stream_stopped`, no console alert yet); reconfiguring Audit or restarting the agent retries from the same cursor, so a record that always panics stops the stream again (ADR-0031); the panic hook logs the code location, thread and a panic id, never the panic message (#79, ADR-0029 decision 1)
- Console: `migrate` applies migration `0027` itself on installs at `0026`: the `bytes` column and the `access_events_bytes` CHECK `NOT VALID` in a short transaction recorded with `0027`'s hash, then `VALIDATE CONSTRAINT` on its own, so the upgrade no longer scans `access_events` under an `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` lock; constraints listed in `DEFERRED_VALIDATIONS` and still `NOT VALID` are validated on every run; final schema unchanged; concurrent `migrate` runs are serialized by an advisory lock (#75, ADR-0028)
- Console: incident dedup keyed by the principal (account name or fingerprint) for every event but `auth_failure`, so fingerprinted OpenLDAP users no longer share one incident; after a false positive, an event with a new `signature.*` signal, a higher score or more rows linked since the mark than the incident's total opens a new incident (#81, ADR-0031)
- Agent: audit records are parsed and converted in isolation on the log-file sources, the MongoDB profiler and the OpenLDAP accesslog (not yet on `pg_stat_statements` and `performance_schema`): a record that makes a connector panic is dropped alone and counted (`audit.records_dropped`, heartbeat metric `audit_record_panics_total`); the OpenLDAP stream locates and skips a record that keeps ending the stream (isolation mode, `audit_records_skipped_total`); streams stop only after 7 panics at one position, more than 8 skips or 64 panics per hour (#83, ADR-0032)
- Agent: PostgreSQL role and account statements (`CREATE` / `ALTER` / `DROP` `ROLE` / `USER` / `GROUP`…) are classified `dcl`, not `ddl`, on the `pg_stat_statements` source, as pgaudit does; the `pg_stat_statements` and `performance_schema` statements are analyzed and converted in isolation, so a statement that makes the agent panic is dropped alone instead of stopping the stream; on `pg_stat_statements` such a statement is cached as poisoned and counted once in `audit_record_panics_total` while it stays in the analysis cache (#88); its later deltas, which #88 dropped, are reported since #90, each as a read of `*` with its calls and rows, built from the counters, role and database only, never treated as the agent's own and never budgeted (#90)
- Agent: the PostgreSQL connector's own table-less statements are recognized as the agent's own on `pg_stat_statements` by their exact stored text (constants as `$n`, the connector's bound parameters present), and only for the agent's role, at top level, in a database where the connector runs them, under the first `queryid` seen there (the same text from another role or database, nested or under another `queryid` is reported); own-account row charges not saved yet are flushed every 30 s (#90)
- Agent: OpenLDAP failed-operation proof re-run at every report with a probe DN unique to each probe (only the latest answer counts), proofs no longer borrowed from nested naming contexts, overlap CSNs persisted across restarts (`v2` cursor, the phase-6 format still read); MongoDB `auditLog` None until a record is parsed in the last 24 h, truncated profiler commands reported with an unknown shape, map collapse fail closed on unobserved levels, application names forgotten on logout; MySQL `check()` fails closed when `APPLICABLE_ROLES` cannot be read; audit log files owned or writable by the agent's account are refused (`audit.log_not_readable`); SCRAM and failed-login intermediates zeroized (#83, ADR-0030, ADR-0032)
- Agent: MySQL / MariaDB audit-log records are grouped per connection id and query id instead of by adjacency, so concurrent sessions whose records interleave no longer have about 12 % of their statements counted twice; a statement split by the poll bound or a log rotation is one event; held statements survive a restart (cursor rewound and replayed); late tables of a statement reported early are reported as a continuation; the grouping state is bounded in bytes (16 MiB pending, 8 MiB of hashes, early reports forgotten after 10 minutes) and its evictions are counted in `audit_pending_evicted_total` (#93)
- Agent: Discovery pacing rotates the PostgreSQL databases and the OpenLDAP naming contexts per scan, as MongoDB did, so one slow object in the first database no longer runs every scan out of time before the later databases or suffixes (#93)
- Agent: a scan stopped by Discovery pacing before its deadline is reported `failed` (`timeout`) instead of `succeeded` when the console does not take the coverage counters (`job_progress.coverage`), so the gap is not hidden (#93)
- Agent: a MariaDB / MySQL audit statement replayed after a restart is timed from its log time, so an agent restarting more often than every 5 minutes no longer pins its cursor to it (#93)
- Agent: a poisoned `pg_stat_statements` statement, whose deltas are reported as reads of `*`, is no longer counted in `audit.records_dropped` nor logged as dropped (#93)

### 🐛 Agent classifiers
- Value-based column classification: birth dates, person names and postal addresses are detected without a column-name hint (age distribution, name lexicon, address structure); broader phone, IBAN, NIR, AWS key and password-hash formats; card and e-mail precision rules (checksum consistency, personal mailboxes only)
- Values are put in Unicode NFC before detection and fingerprinting: fingerprints of decomposed (NFD) non-ASCII values, e.g. accented e-mail addresses, change to those of their composed form
- The `regex` `unicode-case` feature is declared by the classifiers crate (case-insensitive patterns failed in the production build) and every pattern is compiled at agent startup

### ✅ Tests
- End-to-end Audit path on PostgreSQL (pgaudit) and MariaDB (`server_audit` log): `pg_dump` and `mariadb-dump` from the dev clients raise an incident in under 2 minutes (69 s and 58 s in CI), I2 checked on events, incidents, pages and notifications, the agent's own reads never reported (#66)
- End-to-end MongoDB and OpenLDAP targets: I2 on Discovery and Audit, a real `mongodump` and a bulk `ldapsearch` raise an incident, every OpenLDAP principal a fingerprint; password-bearing DCL on PostgreSQL and MariaDB, the passwords nowhere on the console side nor in clear in the targets' logs (#82)
- Console: the per-agent in-flight verification test is deterministic (#84)
