The DataBastion Community Edition code is licensed under Apache 2.0. As provided in its section 6, this license **grants no rights** to the "DataBastion" name or logo.

## Allowed without asking
- Saying that your product or service "uses DataBastion", "is compatible with DataBastion" or "is based on DataBastion"
- Using the name in articles, tutorials, talks or training materials
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under their name

## Requires written permission
- Distributing a **modified** version under the "DataBastion" name (a fork must change its name)
- Offering a hosted service named "DataBastion" or with a name that could be confused with it
- Using the logo in a commercial product

For any request, open a discussion on the repository.
